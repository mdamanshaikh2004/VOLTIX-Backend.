# VOLTIX 12-product catalogue — source notes

The product images in this catalogue are the exact URLs supplied by the project owner. Known products were matched from the visible product image and cross-checked with public product/specification information. For generic/unbranded images, the catalogue intentionally uses a VOLTIX generic product name rather than inventing a manufacturer or model that is not supported by the image.

1. HP 15-dy4013dx — Best Buy image URL; HP/Best Buy specification references were used for the 15.6-inch touch display, 11th Gen i5, 12GB RAM and 256GB SSD.
2. Dell Inspiron 15 3520 — supplied image visually matches the Dell Inspiron 15 3520 product family; the catalogue uses the common i5/8GB/512GB configuration and keeps the model at family level.
3. Apple MacBook Air 13-inch 2020, MWTK2LL/A — supplied image matches the silver 2020 wedge-style MacBook Air; specifications use the 1.1GHz 10th Gen Intel Core i3, 8GB RAM and 256GB SSD configuration.
4. Apple iPhone 16 Pro — supplied image shows the Pro-style iPhone with triple cameras and Dynamic Island; model details use the iPhone 16 Pro family specification.
5. ASUS ROG Phone 7, AI2205 — supplied ASUS image explicitly shows ROG Phone 7; specifications use the 12GB/256GB variant where applicable.
6. ZTE Blade V60 Design, Z2350 — supplied image is the official ZTE Blade V60 Design front image; specifications use the 6GB/256GB variant.
7. Beats Studio3 Wireless — supplied image matches the black Beats Studio3 design; product family specifications are used.
8. VOLTIX RGB Gaming Headset — supplied image shows a wired over-ear gaming headset with illuminated earcup and boom microphone but no identifiable manufacturer/model; no unsupported brand/model is claimed.
9. Sony WH-CH520 — supplied image is the Sony WH-CH520/CZE beige product image; Sony specifications are used.
10. VOLTIX Gaming Controller — supplied image is a generic gaming product image without a visible manufacturer/model; no unsupported manufacturer/model is claimed.
11. Sony DualShock 4 Wireless Controller — supplied image matches the white PlayStation DualShock 4 controller family.
12. VOLTIX Game Console — supplied Getty/iStock image is a generic black game-console stock image without a visible manufacturer/model; no unsupported manufacturer/model is claimed.

Ratings and review counts are initialized to zero because no VOLTIX customer-review dataset was supplied. Inventory values are initial catalogue inventory values and can be changed from Admin Product Management.
